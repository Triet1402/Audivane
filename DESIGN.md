# Interface design

Audivane is a local audio comparison workbench. Make choosing a source,
configuring A/B and listening the primary path.

- Keep source, output and test plan in a persistent sidebar.
- Use Setup, Listen and Evidence views. Keep transport and answer controls stable
  through preparation, loading and trial changes.
- **Prepare test** prepares, verifies and stages paused; **Play** is explicit.
- Put technical reports and exports in Evidence. Keep actionable errors,
  ambiguity and session locks visible.
- Use a contained drawer on narrow screens, with keyboard focus management.
- Offer persisted System, Light and Dark themes. Use self-hosted IBM Plex,
  neutral charcoal/slate surfaces and restrained blue accents.
- Preserve accessible names, contrast, focus indicators and reduced-motion behavior.

Scientific wording must distinguish validity from audibility. Early-ended,
sealed and completed runs must remain visibly distinct. See
[scientific policy](docs/methodology.md) for the behavioral constraints.
