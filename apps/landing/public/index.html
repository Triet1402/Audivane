Bring a track you know.

Import a local audio file and choose the codec chain for each side. Both chains start from the same decoded source.

Prepare a fair comparison.

Audivane aligns and level-matches the pair, verifies the delivered audio, and stages the first trial paused. You decide when to play.

Listen. Choose. Review.

Switch between A, B and X, then decide whether X is A or B. Complete the planned trials and review the results and preparation evidence.

Evidence, with its limits.

Audivane records preparation, delivered-audio identity and fixed-trial ABX results. Numerical checks alone do not prove an audible difference or certify a codec. Your hardware, operating-system processing and listening conditions still matter.
